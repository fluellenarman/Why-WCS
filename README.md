# Why WCS, An essay by Arman Umali


By Arman Umali
# 1. Musical Interpretation
Modern WCS culture emphasizes:
- Hitting musical accents
- Playing with breaks
- Tempo changes
- Lyrical interpretation

The above is why WCS is danced to such a wide variety of Musical Genres
- Blues
- R&B
- Pop
- Soul
- Funk
- Contemporary
**Literally anything in the top 100 since the early 2000s**

WCS tempo ranges from **80-130 BPM**. 
WCS can be slow danced or fast danced.

# 2. An improvisational partner dance

Unlike other dances, WCS is a **Lead-Follow Conversation**. 
Meaning that it's highly improvisational and based on partner connection. Ideal for people who want to/ need to socialize(Touch grass).

## WCS is based on improv and connection between partners.
Other partner dances (ECS, Waltz) are based on choreography.
